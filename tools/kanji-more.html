Build the kanji breakdowns

Joins the breakdowns in tools/kanji-parts.txt with part names from tools/kanji-radicals.txt, checks each split against KRADFILE, and writes the parts into data/kanji-n5.json. Kanji outside N5 also get readings and stroke counts from KANJIDIC2 (.dict/kanjidic2-en-3.6.2.json). Readings the app's words use are always kept; otherwise the first two of each kind.
