Build data/dict.json

Makes the app's offline dictionary from JMdict (.dict/jmdict-eng-3.6.2.json): every common word, every word on the N5 list (.dict/jlpt/n5.csv) and every word the course teaches. Each word gets a frequency score from Tatoeba's Japanese sentences (.dict/jpn_sentences_detailed.tsv). Open it from tools/serve.ps1.

Loading. JMdict is large, so this takes a few seconds.
